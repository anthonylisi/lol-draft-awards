How the sausage was made: every drafted pick, 2019–2025 (1,065 of them), scored against actual NFL production at this league's PPR settings. A "class score" is total points above a startable replacement at each position. Keepers excluded — you have to draft your glory here. Kickers and defenses were spared, as is tradition.

2019 results count current members only. Injuries are bad luck, not bad picks — that's the whole point of the Snakebit Trophy. Disputes may be submitted in writing to the Wooden Spoon Cabinet.
