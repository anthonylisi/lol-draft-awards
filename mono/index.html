Method

How the sausage was made: 1,065 skill-position picks by current members entered into evidence; 35 keepers ruled inadmissible — you have to draft your glory here — and the remaining 1,030 stood trial against actual NFL production at this league's PPR settings. A "class score" is total points above a startable replacement at each position. Kickers and defenses were spared, as is tradition.

2019 results count current members only. Injuries are bad luck, not bad picks — that's the whole point of the Snakebit Trophy. Disputes may be submitted in writing to the Wooden Spoon Cabinet.
